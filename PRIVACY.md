# Privacy

The current privacy disclosure shipped with the simulator and Zoo Kinder iOS
build is maintained in
[`app/public/legal/PRIVACY.txt`](app/public/legal/PRIVACY.txt). It describes the
reference build, not third-party forks or modified deployments.

Distributors must review and replace the disclosure when they change network
behavior, add telemetry, target children, operate a relay or backend, or submit
the app under platform-specific privacy rules.
