ROBOT MODEL

The 3D robot (robot/microzoo/**) is generated from MicroZoo's own microduck_927
CAD export by tools/build_robot_model.py. Third-party reference parts are
replaced by proxy solids; no Pollen 3D asset is shipped. The roller ankle mass
and inertia parameters are carried over from the former roller model and are
listed as an open item in ASSET_LICENSES.md.

GENERATED VOICE ASSETS

The current duck1 through duck4 chirp and wheee WAV files and wawa.wav were
generated on 2026-09-03 as 48 kHz mono PCM from seeded oscillator/noise
recipes. No input audio recording was supplied. The generator is the upstream
sounds/ crate from the Apache-2.0 pollen-robotics/microduck revision
590b986bd8c0d50ae02cb3ea2f59c463b6828168. MicroZoo makes the listed generated
files available under Apache-2.0 to the extent it holds copyright or other
licensable rights. The source distribution records exact seeds, variants, and
commands in app/public/assets/voices/README.md. This provenance statement is
not a warranty that no third-party rights exist.

PROGRAMMATIC REPLACEMENTS

The current Loft 13 and Vintage Room environments, arcade props, and football
surface are generated by project-authored source under Apache-2.0. The former
downloaded Loft/Vintage GLBs, prop GLBs, and ball PNG textures are absent from
the current tree and build. The former voice files, including wawa.mp3, are
likewise replaced by the generated WAV set. Removed assets remain in
pre-removal Git history; do not redistribute that history until the objects are
purged or separately licensed. Cleaning an upstream history cannot revoke
copies already obtained by others.

The nine other deployment policy files are redistributed from the
Apache-2.0-licensed pollen-robotics/microduck project, as detailed in the
source repository's ASSET_LICENSES.md and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

The former experimental policies/run.onnx and Sprint UI are absent from this
build because the project did not have a sufficient redistribution record.
The former wbc/microduck-wbc policy and CSV bundle is likewise absent. Four
project-authored 50 Hz pose motions are generated directly by
src/game/procedural-motions.js under the project source license.
